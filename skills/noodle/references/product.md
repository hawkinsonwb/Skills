# Product planning

1. **Clarify the idea.** Establish the problem, affected users, current experience, and why a change matters. Start from answers already given.
2. **Define user outcomes.** Walk through the intended experience and agree on observable success, including consequential failures and edge cases.
3. **Determine scope.** Choose the smallest useful outcome, boundaries, dependencies, and unresolved product decisions. Discuss technical constraints only when they change that outcome.
4. **Create and validate tasks, if desired.** Draft the problem, scope, acceptance criteria, repository ownership where known, and dependencies. Walk through the draft with the user and incorporate corrections before treating it as agreed.

Ask focused questions at each stage until its decisions are clear. Keep assumptions explicit. Finish with an agreed product summary or actionable tasks, rather than drifting into implementation design.
